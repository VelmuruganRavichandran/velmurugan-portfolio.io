About Me

Hello! I'm Velmurugan, a Data Science graduate from The American College, Madurai Kamaraj University — someone who believes in building skills that compound and solving problems that matter. During my academic journey, I didn't just complete a degree — I took ownership of my learning. From predicting used car prices to analyzing online food order behavior, I’ve applied core concepts of Data Analysis and Model Building to real-world datasets. I also interned at PLC Technologies, where I contributed to diverse projects like Diabetes Prediction, Hotel Room Price Forecasting, Sentiment Analysis, and even Helmet Detection. These projects sharpened my abilities in Data Cleaning, Visualization, and Model Evaluation — skills I now use daily. Outside the classroom, I immersed myself in self-learning: completed a hands-on Data Science Bootcamp on Udemy, attended a national workshop on Cyber Security, and earned a Data Analytics certificate from Accenture via Forage. What sets me apart? I’m not afraid to do the hard things first. In fact, the one decision I got right in college was committing to “Eat That Frog” — tackling high-impact tasks first thing every morning for a full year. Now, I’m ready to bring this mindset and momentum into the world of Data Science — to learn, build, and create impact wherever I go.
